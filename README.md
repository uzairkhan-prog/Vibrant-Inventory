## About Vibrant Inventory

Vibrant Inventory is a modern inventory management system built with Laravel 10. It provides a user-friendly interface for managing products, categories, customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, payments, and more. Designed for small to medium-sized businesses, it helps streamline inventory control and business processes.

---

## Features

- Product and Category Management with full CRUD operations
- Customer and Supplier management
- Sales and Purchase order management
- Payment tracking (including multiple payment methods)
- Expense and Income management
- Ledger system for tracking transactions
- Responsive and clean Blade templates
- Authentication and user management
- Export data functionality (Excel, PDF)
- Modular and extendable Laravel structure
